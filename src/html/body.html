What timescale does “Default Capacity (FTE)” represent?

FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) is a continuous headcount rate — identical for both Weekly and Monthly views. Under the hood, PulsePlan stores each week’s FTE rate and converts it to Days and Hours (1.0 FTE = 5d/wk = 40h/wk; a 4-week month = 20d / 160h, a 5-week month = 25d / 200h).
